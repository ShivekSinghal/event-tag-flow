TIER 3:

MINUTE TO MINUTE
HOW TO PLAY

1. READY, SET, GO
The timer starts when the Game Runner says GO!

2. ROLL THE DICE
Roll the die.
7 or more? You’re in.
6 or less? Sorry, roll again.

3. BALL IT UP
Get the ball from one end of the table to the opposite edge with exactly 7 taps.

4. STACK ATTACK
Your dice total = your cup count.
Rolled an 8? Stack 8 cups. Easy math, right?

5. SHOT & FLIP
Take your shot, then flip your cup and land it upside down.

6. MESSED UP? DO IT AGAIN.
Miss a stage? Repeat that stage until you nail it.

7. BEAT THE CLOCK
Complete the entire sequence before the timer runs out.

AND THE WINNER IS...

Anyone who completes all the stages within the timer wins a PINKREDIBLE.

GROUP ENTRY? BRING IT ON.
Everyone can play, but only the fastest person takes home the Pinkredible.

THE DRINK BIT

The drink component may be alcoholic only where legally and operationally permitted. A non-alcoholic alternative will always be available.


BOMBASTIC

HOW TO PLAY

1. GET IN LINE
5 players. One line. One bomb. Good luck.

2. START THE COUNTDOWN
The Game Runner starts the timer — but keeps the duration a secret.

3. COPY. ADD. PASS.
Player 1 does a move.
Player 2 repeats it and adds a new move.
Player 3 repeats both and adds another.
And so on...

Once it reaches the end of the line, the sequence starts again from the other end.

4. DON’T HOLD THE BOMB
Keep it moving. Deliberately holding onto the bomb for too long = not cool.

5. BOOM!
When the timer goes off, whoever is holding the bomb is ELIMINATED.


6. RESET & REPEAT
The timer resets.
The bomb keeps moving.
Repeat until only one person remains.

WINNER
Last person standing takes the win and the shot!

IMPORTANT

Pass the bomb safely.
No throwing it at people. No deliberately stopping someone from catching it.
Keep it fun. Keep it moving.


LIMBO

HOW TO PLAY:

TAKE THE CHALLENGE At the beginning of your turn, take the designated drink/challenge and get ready for the bar.

GO UNDER! Make your way underneath the limbo bar without touching it or falling.

IT GETS LOWER! Survived the round? Nice. The bar goes lower. And lower. And lower... 😈

FACE UP Your body must remain facing upward while passing underneath the bar.

NO HANDS! Hands cannot be used to support yourself on the floor. You've got to trust the limbo skills!

TOUCH IT OR DROP IT Touch the bar?  Fall down?  You're OUT!

THE WINNER
The last person standing takes the crown — and the Pinkredible!

ISSUE WITH A TISSUE

HOW TO PLAY
BUILD YOUR TEAM Two teams of 5 players line up and get ready for battle.

START WITH THE TISSUE The first player holds the tissue using only their mouth.
Hands are officially out of the game. 😈

PASS IT ON Transfer the tissue from your mouth to the next player's mouth without using your hands.
Sounds easy?
It isn't:)

KEEP IT MOVING Continue passing the tissue down the line until all 5 team members have successfully received it.

LEAVE A LITTLE BEHIND Here's the catch...
After making their transfer, every player must have some piece or residue of the tissue remaining in their mouth.
No evidence? No win!!

DROPPED IT? BIG ISSUE. If the tissue is dropped at any point, the entire team goes back to the beginning.
START. AGAIN. FROM. PLAYER ONE.
No shortcuts. No mercy

Choose your batters wisely.

Time for a ONE-BALL SHOWDOWN. 
One ball. One moment. One chance to win.

GAME RUNNER = FINAL CALL The Game Runner decides whether a delivery is legal, confirms the runs, and records the official score.
Winning team gets a beer each🔥

SPIN THE WHEEL

HOW TO PLAY

One Spin. One Shot.
You get ONE spin of the wheel -make it count! 

Start at the Start
The wheel must be spun from the designated starting position.

Let It Stop!
No stopping, nudging, touching, or helping the wheel. Let it come to a complete and natural stop.

Where You Land = What You Get
The section/number where the pointer lands determines your prize or result.

Tricky Landing?
If the pointer lands ambiguously between two sections, the Game Runner has the final call OR you may be given one re-spin, at the Game Runner’s discretion!

Instant Result!
Your result will be announced immediately after the spin.
